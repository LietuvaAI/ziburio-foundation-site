Skaitmeniniai KnygnešiaiSkaitmeniniai knygnešiai

The nineteenth-century knygnešiai carried prohibited Lithuanian books across borders so that others could read, teach, and pass them on. Their work depended on a wider community of readers, teachers, families, and parishes.

Skaitmeniniai Knygnešiai gives young people a place in that continuing history. Working with books, periodicals, documents, and audiovisual records, they investigate how Lithuanian life was built outside Lithuania. The archive becomes a place to use their language, ask questions of earlier generations, and contribute knowledge others can use.

Digitizing and publishing a source brings responsibilities with it: identifying where it came from, checking an interpretation, and recognizing what remains unknown. Those judgments are part of the apprenticeship, alongside historical and technical skills.

Skaitmeniniai knygnešiai – Žiburio lituanistinės mokyklos inicijuota programa, tęsianti istorinę knygnešių misiją naujoje terpėje.

Kaip spaudos draudimo laikotarpiu knygnešiai užtikrino lietuviško žodžio ir tapatybės išlikimą, taip šiandien moksleiviai, pasitelkdami dirbtinį intelektą ir mobiliuosius įrenginius, perkelia lietuvių diasporos kultūrinę atmintį į skaitmeninę erdvę.

The Žiburio ArchiveArchyvas

The Detroit Lithuanian community’s books, periodicals, and documents record how people continued an intellectual, religious, educational, and civic life far from home. An ownership inscription or a school stamp can reveal a journey that a publication’s title alone would never tell.

The catalogue gives these materials a public point of entry. We care for the originals, document their origins, and connect them with the people and institutions in their histories. Researchers, teachers, and families can then follow those connections, compare accounts, and return to the evidence.

Kiekviena knyga archyve yra atminties vienetas, kuris egzistuoja, turi struktūrą ir atskleidžia tiesą apie žmones, kurie ją sukūrė ir perdavė.

Connecting collections through Lietuva.aiLietuva.ai

A community’s history rarely sits in one collection. A person named in a parish record may also appear in a school publication, a newspaper, or a family’s papers. Lietuva.ai is the wider effort to make those connections legible, with each source’s origin and attribution still visible.

Connecting collections does not make them interchangeable or transfer responsibility away from their custodians. Their identities, rights, and terms of access must remain intact; documented facts must remain distinguishable from proposed connections and machine-generated interpretations.

Lietuva.AI jungia lietuvių kultūros įrašus su žmonėmis, vietomis ir istorijomis, kurias jie liudija. Šaltiniai lieka matomi, atsakomybė — žmogaus rankose.

Learning and creating from our pastMokomės ir kuriame iš savo praeities

The record gives us more than a history to retell. It lets us examine how communities made decisions, what they built together, and which commitments endured. Research, teaching, writing, and new cultural work can grow from those questions.

Save Our Lithuanian Parishes applies this approach to institutions that housed schools, societies, libraries, and archives as well as places of worship. Their histories connect religious life with education, mutual assistance, and civic organization.

The project brings documentary evidence and community knowledge together to ask who built these institutions, how they supported one another, and what happens when they close or change. Understanding that history gives present-day communities a firmer basis for deciding what to carry forward.
