Inheritance

Human intelligence does not develop in isolation. Each generation inherits the language, knowledge, institutions, traditions, and judgments of those who came before it. Through this inheritance, human beings are able to build upon experiences far beyond their own lifetimes.

Culture makes this possible.

Lithuania

For Lithuania, the preservation of cultural memory has repeatedly been a matter of national survival. Across centuries of foreign rule, the suppression of language, occupation, and displacement, Lithuanians sustained their identity through books, schools, churches, families, and institutions built by their own communities.

The Lithuanian diaspora became an essential part of that continuity, preserving and developing cultural, intellectual, and religious life beyond the borders of the Lithuanian state.

The Foundation

Žiburio Foundation exists to ensure that this inheritance remains accessible, intelligible, and alive for future generations.

We preserve original sources, connect dispersed collections, support historical research, and develop new ways to study and transmit cultural memory. Our work brings together archival preservation, education, historical scholarship, and emerging technologies.

The Foundation's public-interest work is to preserve cultural materials with their provenance and context, enable legitimate community participation, develop youth formation and historical inquiry, and support research and public access consistent with rights and institutional obligations. It supports the people and institutions through whom cultural memory remains accountable and alive.

But the purpose is not simply to preserve more information.

It is to preserve the human capacity to understand, interpret, and carry forward what has been inherited.

Grėsmė

Uždaromos parapijos, išsisklaido bendruomenės, o po savininkų mirties knygos ir archyvai prarandami. Kartu su fiziniais objektais nyksta jų kontekstas – žinios apie tai, kas juos kūrė, kam jie buvo svarbūs.

Didelė dalis archyvų yra 50–80 metų senumo, DP leidiniai spausdinti prasta kokybe. Kartu nyksta karta, galėjusi paaiškinti kontekstą. Fiksuojame ne tik objektus, bet ir jų istoriją – kol dar įmanoma.

Our purposeMūsų tikslas

Cultural continuity requires more than preservation.Tai nėra vien archyvavimas.

An archive can preserve a document. It cannot, by itself, preserve the language needed to read it, the historical context needed to understand it, or the institutions and relationships that once gave it meaning.

These must also be transmitted.

Žiburio Foundation approaches cultural memory as a living inheritance: a body of knowledge, experience, language, faith, and institutional life accumulated across generations.

We work to preserve both the historical record and the human capacity to engage with it.

This means connecting sources that have become dispersed, making historical evidence accessible for research and education, and preparing young people to assume responsibility for the cultural inheritance they receive.

Tai – struktūruotos atminties kūrimas: objektai siejami su jų kilme, istoriniu kontekstu, žmonėmis ir įvykiais, atkuriant tęstinę, gyvą kultūrinę sistemą, kuri gali būti perduodama toliau.

Archyvas gali išsaugoti dokumentą. Bet jis pats neišsaugo nei kalbos, kuria dokumentas skaitomas, nei konteksto, kuriuo jis suprantamas, nei įstaigų ir ryšių, kurie jam davė prasmę. Visa tai taip pat turi būti perduota.

The question we start fromKlausimas, nuo kurio pradedame

What is worth the cost of carrying forward?Kas verta, kad būtų nešama toliau?

Cultures do not consist of everything people produce. They also consist of what people deliberately preserve, repeat, teach, honor, dispute, or refuse to surrender. A prayer book carried through exile, a school established by displaced families, minutes recording an institution's difficult decisions, or a song taught to children after language loss is evidence not simply of content but of commitment.

Preferences can be expressed in a questionnaire; commitments often become legible only through costly choices. Culture provides evidence of how values operate under constraint: scarcity, migration, persecution, mortality, institutional conflict, and intergenerational obligation.

Istoriniai knygnešiai nešė knygas per sienas ir draudimus, kad lietuvių kalba ir kultūra neišnyktų. Šiandien grėsmė kyla ne iš draudimo, o iš parapijų uždarymo, kartos pamiršimo ir knygų išmetimo po savininkų mirties.

Nešė ją knygnešiai, uždraudus lietuvišką spaudą. Nešė mokytojai DP stovyklose, netekus valstybės. Nešė tėvai ir parapijos Detroite — statydami mokyklas, chorus, skautų draugoves, leidyklas.

Knygnešiai nesaugojo — nešė. Tremtinių karta neistorino — atstatė.

Lithuanian cultural history offers repeated examples of transmission under constraint. The knygnešiai maintained a printed language in the face of imperial restriction; displaced persons and later diaspora generations recreated newspapers, schools, parishes, scouting organizations, and civic associations outside an occupied homeland. These institutions transmitted far more than facts. They embodied judgments about language, freedom, faith, belonging, duty, and what future generations should inherit.

Their value lies in connections: a book's ownership mark, a teacher's name, a parish bulletin, a child's participation, an institution's decision, a later public controversy. No one artifact contains the full account.

Be Valančiaus parapijų tinklo — nebūtų knygnešių. Be knygnešių — nebūtų raštingos tautos, kuri 1918 m. atkūrė valstybę. Be tos valstybės mokytojų — nebūtų DP stovyklų mokyklų. Be DP mokyklų — nebūtų Detroito „Žiburio“.

Kiekviena grandis laikė tą, kuri ėjo po jos. Nė vienas daiktas neneša viso pasakojimo: knygos įrašas, mokytojo vardas, parapijos biuletenis, vaiko dalyvavimas, įstaigos sprendimas.

How do human communities create and sustain obligations across generations, and can AI systems recognize those obligations without converting them into generic preferences?

Istoriniai knygnešiai saugojo lietuvišką žodį. Šiandien turime išsaugoti lietuvišką atmintį. Ji bus išsaugota tik tada, kai bus ir perduota — per jaunimą.

Founding statementSteigimo žodis

Why Žiburio Foundation existsKodėl fondas gyvuoja

Our work begins with Lithuanian cultural memory. The questions it addresses are universal.

Mūsų darbas prasideda nuo lietuviškos atminties. Klausimai, kuriuos jis kelia, yra visų.

Every generation inherits more than the knowledge it can acquire for itself. It inherits languages, institutions, traditions, relationships, and ways of understanding the world that have developed over centuries.

This accumulated inheritance makes human civilization possible.

But inheritance is never automatic. It depends on people willing to preserve what came before them, understand its meaning, and carry it into circumstances their predecessors could not have imagined.

Lithuanian history offers an extraordinary example of this responsibility. Across occupation, displacement, and generations of life beyond the homeland, cultural continuity was sustained through the deliberate work of individuals and communities.

Today, artificial intelligence is transforming the ways knowledge is produced, encountered, and transmitted. This makes the preservation of authentic cultural sources, their contexts, and the human institutions responsible for them more important—not less.

Žiburio Foundation was established on the conviction that cultural memory is essential infrastructure for human intelligence and that responsibility for its future must remain in human hands.

Be kalbos nėra tautos.

Tautos atmintis — ne istorija ir ne archyvas. Tai gyvas ryšių tinklas, jungiantis žmones, vietas, įvykius ir tekstus per laiką.

Kiekviena karta paveldi daugiau, negu pati gali sužinoti: kalbą, įstaigas, papročius, ryšius ir mąstymo būdus, kuriuos sudėjo šimtmečiai. Tas paveldas nepereina savaime. Jis pereina tik per žmones, kurie ryžtasi jį išsaugoti, suprasti ir nunešti toliau.

Lietuvos istorija tai liudija. Per okupacijas, tremtį ir gyvenimą svetur tęstinumą laikė sąmoningas žmonių ir bendruomenių darbas.

Šiandien dirbtinis intelektas keičia tai, kaip žinios kuriamos, randamos ir perduodamos. Todėl tikri šaltiniai, jų kontekstas ir žmonės, kurie už juos atsako, darosi svarbesni — ne mažiau svarbūs.

Žiburio Foundation įsteigtas iš įsitikinimo, kad tautos atmintis yra pamatas, o atsakomybė už jos ateitį turi likti žmogaus rankose.

Our work depends on individuals, families, institutions, and communities who believe that cultural memory deserves careful stewardship and a future beyond any single generation.

Support helps preserve historical collections, develop public access, train young people, and build the research and educational resources needed to carry this work forward.

The work produces immediate public value: more accessible archives, more accountable historical knowledge, stronger community stewardship, and young people better able to reason from the records they inherit.
