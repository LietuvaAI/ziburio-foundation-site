After Lithuanian school, there is still work to do—and a place for its graduates to do it.

In Skaitmeniniai Knygnešiai, students catalogue books, scan pages, and check the results against the originals. The language and history they learned at school become part of working with their community’s own collections.

The name recalls the knygnešiai who carried Lithuanian books during the nineteenth-century Russian press ban. Today’s students are learning to work with these sources as new technologies change how people read and use them.

Skaitmeniniai knygnešiai – Žiburio lituanistinės mokyklos inicijuota programa, tęsianti istorinę knygnešių misiją naujoje terpėje.

Gimnazijos amžius yra paskutinis etapas, kai lituanistinė mokykla gali giliai įtraukti jaunimą. Daugelyje mokyklų po 8 klasės mokiniai pasitraukia. Nuo 8-os klasės iki universiteto – nieko.

Programa prasideda ties slenksčiu: mokinys eina į suaugusiojo gyvenimą, o tautos atmintis — į pasaulį, kuriame žinios bus randamos, suprantamos ir perduodamos naujaip. Du klausimai neatskiriami vienas nuo kito.

At the archiveDarbas

Seven Žiburio Lithuanian School graduates are digitizing and cataloguing the archive at Divine Providence Lithuanian Church: approximately 4,000 books and historical publications. They also research the people, parishes, and institutions connected to those records.

Through Save Our Lithuanian Parishes, they help document 155 parishes and other institutions across the United States. Materials from other communities are already taking the work beyond Detroit.

They ask what these communities built, why some survived and others disappeared, what has been lost, and what may still be carried forward.

Komandoje dirba septyni Žiburio mokyklos abiturientai, Dievo Apvaizdos parapijos nariai. Jie yra tos tradicijos, kurią saugo, paveldėtojai. Dirba šeštadieniais nuo 2025 m. rudens.

Kartu su „Save Our Lithuanian Parishes“ jie padeda aprašyti 155 JAV lietuvių parapijas ir kitas įstaigas.

From the shelves to the recordNuo lentynos iki įrašo

Before a book becomes a catalogue entry, students work through the material together: sorting, identifying publications, and recording basic bibliographic details.Prieš knygai tampant katalogo įrašu, mokiniai drauge rūšiuoja medžiagą, atpažįsta leidinius ir užrašo pagrindinius bibliografinius duomenis.

Learning to read the evidenceKodėl jaunimas

The interns have catalogued books and scanned their pages. They now check AI suggestions against pages they scanned themselves.

In the review workspace, they can confirm or correct a name or category, or flag something they cannot resolve. Each decision stays linked to the page. An intern’s review does not automatically approve a record for publication.

The point is to learn when the system is right, when it is wrong, and when to ask for another review. Work with other media is planned next.

Stažuotojai katalogavo knygas ir skenavo jų puslapius. Dabar pagal tuos pačius puslapius tikrina dirbtinio intelekto pasiūlymus.

Peržiūros aplinkoje jie gali patvirtinti ar pataisyti vardą arba kategoriją, o jei nėra tikri – pažymėti, kad reikia kito žmogaus peržiūros. Sprendimas lieka susietas su puslapiu. Stažuotojo peržiūra savaime nėra leidimas skelbti įrašą.

Svarbu išmokti pastebėti, kada sistema teisi, kada klysta ir kada reikia pagalbos. Darbas su kitomis medijomis numatytas vėliau.

A place in the communityKita karta

Finishing Lithuanian school need not mean leaving its community. The internship gives graduates a reason to return and work together on something other people can use.

A book printed in a displaced-persons camp offers a starting point: who was it for, what did it teach, and why did someone keep it? Students need time, guidance, and access to the books to pursue questions like these.

Stažuotojai įgyja technologinius įgūdžius, archyvinio darbo pagrindus ir gebėjimą analizuoti istorinius šaltinius. Bet svarbiausia – formuoja asmeninį ryšį su lietuviška tapatybe.

Kai gimnazistas katalogizuoja DP stovyklos knygą, kurią jo senelis galėjo laikyti rankose – jis tampa knygnešiu kitoje epochoje.

Jie suvokia save ne kaip pasyvius kultūros vartotojus, o kaip aktyvius saugotojus.

The Žiburio ArchiveArchyvas

The Detroit Lithuanian community’s books, periodicals, and documents record how people continued an intellectual, religious, educational, and civic life far from home. An ownership inscription or a school stamp can reveal a journey that a publication’s title alone would never tell.

The catalogue gives these materials a public point of entry. We care for the originals, document their origins, and connect them with the people and institutions in their histories. Researchers, teachers, and families can then follow those connections, compare accounts, and return to the evidence.

Kiekviena knyga archyve yra atminties vienetas, kuris egzistuoja, turi struktūrą ir atskleidžia tiesą apie žmones, kurie ją sukūrė ir perdavė.

Diasporos archyvai yra visos lietuvių tautos paveldas.

Connecting collections through Lietuva.aiLietuva.ai

A community’s history rarely sits in one collection. A person named in a parish record may also appear in a school publication, a newspaper, or a family’s papers. Lietuva.ai is the wider effort to make those connections legible, with each source’s origin and attribution still visible.

Connecting collections does not make them interchangeable or transfer responsibility away from their custodians. Their identities, rights, and terms of access must remain intact; documented facts must remain distinguishable from proposed connections and machine-generated interpretations.

Lietuva.AI jungia lietuvių kultūros įrašus su žmonėmis, vietomis ir istorijomis, kurias jie liudija. Šaltiniai lieka matomi, atsakomybė — žmogaus rankose.

Learning and creating from our pastMokomės ir kuriame iš savo praeities

Save Our Lithuanian Parishes documents the churches and community institutions built by Lithuanians in the United States. Many also housed schools, societies, libraries, and archives.

The project brings records and community knowledge together: who built these places, what happened in them, and what remains when they close. That history can inform research, teaching, writing, and decisions about the collections left behind.

JAV lietuvių parapijų registras, kuriame aprašytos 155 parapijos ir kitos įstaigos.

Parapijos buvo ne vien maldos namai. Jose veikė mokyklos, draugijos, bibliotekos, archyvai — jos laikė lietuvišką gyvenimą per kartas. Uždaromos parapijos, išsisklaido bendruomenės, o kartu su pastatais nyksta ir jų istorija.
