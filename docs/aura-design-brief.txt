Žiburio Foundation — three homepage design studies

Create three visually distinct homepage concepts for a small Lithuanian cultural foundation. These are review prototypes, not a production launch. Keep the same content in each so the owner can judge the design, not changing words. The owner is not a designer: give her visual choices, not configuration chores.

The Foundation connects cultural inheritance with young people doing real work. It is not an AI startup. Show real books, hands, students and archival materials; no generated people, stock photographs or invented software interfaces. Keep existing Hanken Grotesk typography and the original Žiburio mark with Oswald two-line wordmark. English brand: Žiburio Foundation. Lithuanian: Žiburio Fondas. Logo assets and approved photographs will be supplied separately. Until supplied, use clearly labeled image placeholders, not substitutes.

Common content:
Hero: Memory. Inheritance. Continuity.
Mission: To ensure that Lithuanian cultural knowledge, memory, faith, language, institutions, and archives continue to illuminate future generations.
Projects, only these three:
1. Skaitmeniniai Knygnešiai — the internship. Students catalogue books, scan pages, and check records against the originals. Link to the Foundation's program explanation and the students' work in the Žiburio Archive.
2. Žiburio Archive. Books, periodicals and documents from the Lithuanian diaspora, with photographs and catalogue records. External link: https://archyvas.ziburioltmokykla.org/
3. Save Our Lithuanian Parishes (SOLP). Research into Lithuanian churches and community institutions in the United States. External link: https://saveourlithuanianparishes.org/
The Knygnešių book is a later project. Do not add it, a coming-soon card, or a blog yet.
Contact: info@ziburiofoundation.org. No director's name or title.
Support: link to a short Support page. A donation service is not established; do not invent a donation URL or working payment flow.
Footer: black background, white text; email, Archive and SOLP links; © 2026 Žiburio Foundation; A Michigan nonprofit · established 2026.

Direction A: a contemporary cultural institution. Confident type, one powerful documentary photograph, precise alignment, compact project explanations. Calm but memorable.
Direction B: a youth-led working archive. An intentionally composed sequence of real photographs, book covers and page details with energetic cropping. Clear, readable and approachable; not a collage of random stickers.
Direction C: a digital exhibition. Give the books and their human stories presence through scale, deliberate transitions and restrained interaction. Avoid generic SaaS cards, excessive borders or decorative effects.

Constraints: keep the existing font and mark; navy, white and black with sparing yellow if needed. No green accents, tricolour divider, juosta, folk-pattern decoration, rotated labels, giant flat colour slabs, fake statistics, testimonials or taxonomy. No mentions of Lietuva.ai, CultureNet or Labs. No tax-exemption or deductibility claims. Avoid repetition: each section has one job. Do not shrink headings or create tiny body type. Keep reading order natural and align shared edges. Responsive mobile layout, keyboard focus and reduced-motion support are required.

Bilingual requirement: retain EN/LT navigation and existing independent Lithuanian copy when source files are supplied. Never translate or overwrite it automatically. The production site uses x-en and x-lt elements with CSS switching; don't silently discard either language. English-first visual studies may leave explicitly marked Lithuanian slots rather than invent translations.

Output: three distinct complete homepage previews for comparison, with a one-sentence explanation of each direction. Do not publish to the Foundation's domain or alter its repository. No subscription purchase or hosting migration is authorized.
