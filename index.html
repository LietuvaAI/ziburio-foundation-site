01 How we work Now

Every source passes through the same four steps.

  1. STEP 01

    Digitize

    The source is photographed, catalogued and preserved.

  2. STEP 02

    Extract

    AI reads it and identifies the people, places, institutions and events it mentions.

  3. STEP 03

    Review

    People check every identification, and uncertain cases are held for human judgment.

  4. STEP 04

    Link

    Each confirmed fact is tied to the page it appears on, so any claim can be traced back and verified.

03 The apprenticeship Now

Skaitmeniniai Knygnešiai (Digital Book Carriers)

Much of the work is done by young people from the community. Through Skaitmeniniai Knygnešiai, its apprenticeship in cultural inheritance, high-school interns build their Lithuanian and learn the responsible use of AI by working directly with primary sources.

Seven student interns, scanning and cataloguing the archive's books.

05 Our thinking · research and next steps, labeled

How will we prepare our memory for the world that is coming?

How will we prepare our young people to live responsibly within it?

AI can now speak fluently about any culture, but fluency is not truth. A culture is represented accurately only when its own records are preserved with their sources, context and connections intact, so that meaning is drawn from evidence rather than generated. Communities whose memory exists in this form will be understood on their own terms.

The human being remains responsible for memory. AI helps us see more within it.

  • Žiburio Foundation cannot become an AI company.
  • The community holds its own memory, governs access to it, builds its own intelligence from it, and does not lose it.
  • Representation is not inheritance.
  • A corpus is not a culture.
