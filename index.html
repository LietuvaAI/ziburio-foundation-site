About

Memory and intelligence, together

A new technological era asks two questions of a cultural community:

How will we prepare our memory for the world that is coming?

And:

How will we prepare our young people to live responsibly within it?

Žiburio Foundation was created around those two questions.

Žiburio Foundation is rooted in people, memory and inheritance. It stewards cultural materials and the relationships around them; creates opportunities for young people to encounter original sources; supports the work of preserving, understanding and transmitting Lithuanian cultural memory; and keeps responsibility for that inheritance in human hands.

The Foundation protects the human responsibility. It asks what should be remembered, who is responsible for it, what rights accompany it, and how it should be handed to another generation.

Our thinking

AI should extend the human encounter with memory, not replace it.

An archive should not become merely a dataset.

A young person should not become merely a consumer of machine-generated answers.

And cultural memory should not be stripped from the people, places and relationships that gave it meaning.

Instead, young people can work directly with the record: cataloging it, questioning it, tracing relationships, identifying people and places, learning its history, and using increasingly powerful computational tools to investigate what it contains.

The technology becomes an instrument for deeper encounter.

The archive becomes a place of learning.

And cultural inheritance becomes active again.

This is why the work begins with both memory and youth.

We are preparing archives so that they remain intelligible, connected and trustworthy in an AI-mediated world.

At the same time, we are preparing young people to enter that world with the skills to use powerful technologies without surrendering judgment, authorship, responsibility or their connection to the cultures they inherit.

The human being remains responsible for memory. AI helps us see more within it.

Žiburio Foundation carries the responsibility of inheritance.

What stays human

Our thinking

Žiburio Foundation cannot become an AI company.

The community holds its own memory, governs access to it, builds its own intelligence from it, and does not lose it.

We do not scale by exporting Lithuanian culture. We scale by exporting a way for communities to remain responsible for their own culture while gaining the power of AI.

Representation is not inheritance.

A corpus is not a culture.
