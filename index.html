Our WorkMūsų darbai About the FoundationApie fondą

Memory is not simply a record of the past. It is how a nation, how a civilization, carries itself into the future.

Žiburio Foundation exists to keep Lithuanian cultural inheritance active in the lives of those who receive it. Books and archives connect us to the language, faith, institutions, and judgments through which earlier generations built a common life.

We connect those sources with the people and histories they belong to, and bring young people into the work of interpreting them and making something new. As AI changes how knowledge is encountered, this inheritance must remain grounded in its own evidence and accountable to the communities that carry it.

Žiburio Fondas saugo, skaitmenina ir aprašo Lietuvos diasporos archyvus, leidinius, sakytinę istoriją ir kitas kultūros vertybes.

Technology that helps us read further.Technologija padeda skaityti giliau.

Digital photography brings the source into view. AI can assist with transcription and translation, identify names and places, and suggest connections across collections. Human review checks those suggestions against the original; linked records let another reader trace a claim back to its source.Skaitmeninė fotografija atveria šaltinį. Dirbtinis intelektas gali padėti perrašyti ir versti tekstą, atpažinti vardus bei vietas, pasiūlyti ryšius tarp rinkinių. Žmogus tikrina pagal originalą, o susieti įrašai leidžia skaitytojui grįžti nuo teiginio prie jo šaltinio.
